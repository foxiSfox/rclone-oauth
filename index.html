Personal Drive Backup

A personal integration powered by rclone for backing up your own files to Google Drive.

This application is used solely by its owner to access their own Google Drive. It is not open for registration by other users.

Purpose

The application allows its owner to back up selected files from their own devices or server to their Google Drive, verify backups, and restore files when needed.

Google Drive access

Authorization is handled through Google OAuth. Access is used for backup operations configured by the owner in rclone. Depending on the permissions granted and commands selected, this may include reading files and their metadata, as well as creating, updating, and deleting files and folders.

Selecting a backup folder in rclone does not, by itself, restrict the permissions granted by Google: their scope is determined during authorization. Access can be revoked in the Google Account settings.

Data and privacy

Backups are stored in the owner's Google Drive. This website provides information about the application only and does not accept files or access tokens.
